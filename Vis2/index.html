Disasters Within Australia

The following visualisation depicts disasters which have occured within Australia from 1900 to 2025 through a symbol map. The visualisation then moves in displaying various aspects of disasters through Australia from 1620 - 2022.

Temporal Display of Disasters

The temporal display demonstrates that disaster occurences have increased over time with a peak which occured within 1998. We can also see that most occurences of disasters occur in January, followed by February and December which also align with Australia Summer. Therefore, it may indicate seasonal correlations within Australia's seasons.

Interactivty within the line chart

The most frequent disaster which occurs within Australia fall under Extreme Weather events. These events range from a few disaster types which include, Flood, Storm, Cyclone, Maritime/Coastal, Tornado.
