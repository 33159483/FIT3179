Disasters Within Australia

The following visualisation depicts disasters which have occured within Australia from 1900 to 2025. The visualisation demonstrates a clear most frequent event with Extreme Weather events within Australia. These events notably include Cyclone Tracey & the Black Friday Bushfires, which are disasters entrechned within the history of Australia.

Temporal Display of Disasters

The temporal display demonstrates that disaster occurences have increased over time with a peak which occured within 1998 and 2007 (8 disasters). We can also see that most occurences of disasters occur in January, followed by February and December which also align with Australia Summer. Therefore, it may indicate seasonal correlations within Australia's seasons.

Interactivty within the line chart

The most frequent disaster which occurs within Australia fall under Extreme Weather events. These events range from a few disaster types which include, Flood, Storm, Cyclone, Maritime/Coastal, Tornado.
