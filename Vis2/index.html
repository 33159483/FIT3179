Disasters Within Australia

The following visualisation depicts disasters which have occurred within Australia from 1900 to 2025. The visualisation demonstrates a clear most frequent event with Extreme Weather events within Australia. These events notably include Cyclone Tracy & the Black Friday Bushfires, which are entrenched within the history of Australia.
